ESP32 Board Selection
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Three ESP32 boards available:
      Wemos D1 R32        ──  original board, Uno form factor
      ESP32-WROOM-32      ──  standard 38 pin, CP2102 USB chip
      ESP32-WROOM-32UE    ──  selected for Ghostwave
  - WROOM-32UE chosen because:
      External IPEX/U.FL antenna connector for future WiFi/BT
      Clean pin labels
      Same GPIO layout as all previous work
  - WROOM-32UE requires BOOT button hold during upload
    Does not auto-reset into download mode
  - Sequence: run upload → hold BOOT when "Connecting..."
    appears → release when progress bars start

RF Capabilities Confirmed
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - CC1101 range: 300MHz - 928MHz, not locked to 433MHz
  - Common targets:
      433MHz  ──  car keys, garage doors, weather stations
      315MHz  ──  older US market remotes
      868MHz  ──  European IoT, smart meters
      915MHz  ──  US IoT, LoRa
  - IR not supported by CC1101
    Requires separate module (e.g. TSOP38238) — future addition
  - Potentiometer frequency control planned:
      analogRead mapped across 300-928MHz range
      Real time frequency adjustment while scanning

DEBUGGING PROCESS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Upload Issue Resolved
  - WROOM-32UE does not auto-reset into download mode
  - Fix: hold BOOT button when "Connecting..." appears
  - Uploads reliably with this method confirmed

CC1101 Detection — Full Debugging Log
  - Tried CSN on GPIO5, GPIO13, GPIO15  ──  all failed
  - Tried GDO0 on GPIO2, GPIO25        ──  irrelevant for detection
  - Tried MOSI/MISO physically swapped  ──  failed
  - Tried external power supply         ──  failed
  - Tried direct ESP32 3.3V power       ──  failed
  - Continuity tested all SPI wires     ──  all confirmed good
  - Voltage on CC1101 VCC confirmed: 3.25V

Critical Discovery — Power Supply Battery
  - External power supply battery was not connected
  - Green LED on supply was lit but no stable output
  - Connected battery — CC1101 still failed to detect
  - Switched to direct ESP32 3.3V — still failed

MISO Voltage Test
  - Multimeter on CC1101 MISO pin while ESP32 running
  - Reads 0.05V — essentially 0V
  - CC1101 completely unresponsive on SPI

CC1101 Wiring Verified
  - JST connector used on CC1101 module
  - 6 wires confirmed: VCC, GND, SCK, MOSI, MISO, CSN
  - GDO0 and GDO2 intentionally unconnected for this test
  - All 6 wires continuity tested and confirmed
  - Wiring is correct — module is the problem

CC1101 Verdict
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - All wiring confirmed correct and continuous
  - Power confirmed at 3.25V
  - MISO reads 0V — chip completely unresponsive
  - Conclusion: CC1101 module is dead/DOA
  - 2x replacement modules already ordered
